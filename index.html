How to use it
  1. 1Screenshot a fresh puzzle. Nothing placed on it yet, no cats and no crosses. A half solved board confuses the colour reading and the answer comes out wrong. If you have already started, clear the board in the app first.
  2. 2Upload it here. Pick the file, drag it in, or paste it.
  3. 3Choose how much help you want. One cat at a time, a few squares ruled out, or the whole thing solved.

How it solves the board
  1. One cat in every row and every column.
  2. One cat in every colour region.
  3. No two cats touching, including corner to corner.

No cropping needed. The board is found by looking for the big block of equal-sized coloured squares.
